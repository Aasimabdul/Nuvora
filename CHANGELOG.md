# Changelog

All notable changes to Nuvora. Versions before 1.0.0 were test releases and have been retired from the Releases page; their highlights are kept here.

## 1.0.0

First stable release. It includes everything from the 0.x series:

- Search across YouTube Music, Audius and JioSaavn with a source selector, Songs and Playlists search tabs, and support for pasted or shared YouTube / YouTube Music links.
- Media3 player with floating mini-player, Now Playing screen, seek bar, shuffle, repeat, playback speed and background playback with notification and lock-screen controls.
- "Up next" queue sheet with tap-to-play, reorder, swipe-to-remove, Play next and Add to queue.
- Favourites, recently played, play counts, local playlists and best-effort import of public YouTube Music and JioSaavn playlists.
- Sleep timer (presets and custom 1 to 720 minutes), equalizer presets, custom five-band levels and saved user presets.
- 16 themes including AMOLED Black, Pearl Light and Material You Dynamic colour on Android 12+.
- Direct downloads for sources that provide a downloadable audio URL, with separate artwork download.
- Back from any screen returns to Home first; short crossfade between main screens.
- Startup hardening: the notification permission prompt and MediaSession binding are deferred until the first screen is shown.

## 0.x history

- **0.5.7**: startup stability fixes, fixed Home discovery rendering, thumbnail parsing and fallbacks, persistent library metadata.
- **0.5.5**: compact Home, updated wordmark, provider icons in the source selector, larger mini-player.
- **0.5.4**: Audius trending endpoint, infinite scroll, pull-to-refresh, loading skeletons, state kept across rotation.
- **0.4.0**: in-app playback of YouTube Music audio through NewPipe Extractor.
