Good to know.

A few quick answers before you install.

Is Nuvora free and open source?

Yes. Nuvora is free to download and its source code is published under the GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPL-3.0).

Which devices can run Nuvora?

Nuvora's Android app requires Android 8.0 (API 26) or newer. Check the release notes for the exact build you download.

Will every searched song play in the app?

No. A source may return catalogue information without a compatible playable stream. Audius has supported public-stream playback; YouTube Music and JioSaavn support is unofficial and best-effort. Spotify, SoundCloud and Bandcamp are external-service hand-offs in Nuvora.

Does Nuvora need an account?

Nuvora itself does not require an account. A selected third-party music service may have its own account, region or usage requirements.

Where can I report a bug or help improve Nuvora?

Open an issue on GitHub with your Nuvora version, Android version, selected source and a short description of what happened. Do not share passwords, private tokens or account credentials.
